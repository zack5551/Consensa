# Sports questions from day 1: rules

*LEX, 2026-11-18. zack decided sports go live at launch (reverses "no sports at launch"). Not legal advice. ⚖️ = for a lawyer's eye. These add to the [build guardrails](consensa-build-guardrails.md).*

## Why sports need extra care
Free calls with no prizes aren't gambling. The specific risk is **Criminal Code s. 202(1)(f)**: providing "information intended for use in connection with" betting. Live, sportsbook-style probabilities on games could look like a free odds service for bettors. Everything below keeps Cansensa looking like **fan opinion and forecasting**, not betting information.

## Allowed
- **Outcomes and season questions:**
  - "Will the Leafs make the playoffs?"
  - "Who wins the Grey Cup?"
  - "Will Canada medal in men's hockey?"
  - Awards (Hart Trophy, MVP).
  - Trades and signings, when the result is confirmed by an official source.
- **Game-winner questions, pre-game only.** Calls close **before the start** (puck drop, kick-off, first pitch). Results are shown after the final, official result.
- Canadian focus: NHL, CFL, MLS and CPL, Raptors, Blue Jays, PWHL, national teams, the Olympics and the World Cup 2026 legacy.

## Not allowed
- **In-game or live questions,** or anything that changes during play.
- **Betting-shaped markets:** point spreads, puck lines, over/unders, totals, exact scores, parlays, "first goal scorer", and other prop bets.
- **Individual player stat props** ("Will McDavid score 2+ points tonight?"). Season awards are fine.
- **Amateur, youth, junior (CHL), university or high-school sports.** Integrity risk; Ontario's regulator bars betting on most of these.
- **Odds formats:** decimal (2.40), American (+150) or fractional. Show **percentages only**: "62% of Cansensa fans say yes."
- **Any comparison with sportsbook lines,** "value", "edge", "beat the book", "lock", "sharp" or "picks".
- **The AI never** recommends a call, flags "value", or says who will win. It may explain why the crowd moved ("after the injury report…").

## Words
| Use | Never use |
|---|---|
| call, forecast, the crowd, fans expect, probability, accuracy, resolves | bet, wager, odds, line, spread, parlay, lock, pick, payout, stake, favourite/underdog *as odds*, "action" |

The standard label shows on every sports question: "Free to play. No cash value. No purchase necessary." Add "Fan forecasts, not betting advice."

## Data and partners
- **Never sell, license or feed sports probabilities to sportsbooks, odds services, betting affiliates or tipsters.** No public API of live sports numbers. Any sports-data deal goes to LEX first; selling to operators would also need gaming supplier registration.
- Sportsbook, casino and betting ads and affiliate links stay banned, and sports pages get extra ad-category blocking.
- **Results come from official sources** (league sites, box scores). Facts are free to use. **Don't scrape paid data feeds** (Sportradar, Stats Perform) or copy league content.

## Names, logos and likeness
- Team, league and player **names** can be used to describe facts ("Will the Oilers win?").
- **No league or team logos, jerseys or player photos** without a licence. Text or neutral colours only.
- A footer on sports pages: "Not affiliated with or endorsed by any league, team or player." ⚖️
- No athletes or influencers who appeal to minors in sports marketing (existing guardrail).

## Leaderboards and prizes
- Sports accuracy leaderboards are fine (Brier score).
- **No prizes, rewards or "sports pick'em contests" with anything of value.** Still off (s. 206).

What Canada thinks happens next.

Consensa (working name) is a free, bilingual forecasting app. Every day, Canadians make five quick calls on the economy, weather, housing and culture. The crowd's answer shows up instantly, split by province, and everyone builds an accuracy record.

Free to play. No money, no points to buy and no prizes. Results are probabilities, never odds. The AI explains what changed and never recommends a call. Questions, numbers and usernames here are illustrative.
